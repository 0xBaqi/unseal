Welcome to Unseal Confidential Document Vault

Unseal binds confidential enterprise documents to on-chain NEAR NEP-171 NFT access passes. To protect confidentiality, real document contents and sensitive space directories are shielded behind cryptographic wallet authentication.

1. Gasless Authentication
Sign a single-use NEP-413 cryptographic challenge with zero gas fees.
2. Live On-Chain Gating
Server checks NFT ownership against NEAR RPC at block finality on every request.
3. Zero-Stale Revocation
When an access pass is transferred, previous owners are instantly denied access.
